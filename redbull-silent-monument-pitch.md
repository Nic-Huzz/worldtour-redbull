## SLIDE 6: INTRODUCING SILENT DISCO

**The most shareable, accessible, and scalable sober experience format on earth.**

Silent disco replaces speakers with wireless headphones. Everyone dances to the same DJ — but from the outside, it's silent. Three channels. You choose your vibe.

**Why it's perfect for this cultural moment:**

- **No alcohol required by design** — the experience IS the headphones and the music
- **Inherently viral** — the visual of hundreds of people dancing in silence is irresistible content. Every single person who walks by films it.
- **Zero noise complaints** — you can do it anywhere. A park. A rooftop. A museum. A monument.
- **Daytime-friendly** — sunset sessions, morning raves, afternoon pop-ups
- **Radically inclusive** — three channels means three genres, three moods, three doorways in
- **Low infrastructure** — no stage, no sound system, no noise permits. Just headphones and a transmitter.
- **Participatory by nature** — the audience IS the show (Red Bull's DNA)

This is the only music format where the venue can be *anywhere* and the content creates itself.

## SLIDE 8: THE CONCEPT — RED BULL SILENT MONUMENT TOUR

**Red Bull creates a new cultural format: silent disco experiences at the world's most iconic monuments.**

### The Format
- **Duration**: 60-90 minute pop-up experience
- **Setup**: Red Bull branded mobile DJ unit rolls up to a monument. 200-1,000 wireless headphones distributed.
- **Channels**: 3 curated music channels
  - Channel 1: Local DJ (city-specific flavor)
  - Channel 2: Red Bull artist / curated playlist
  - Channel 3: Crowd-voted wildcard / conscious beats
- **Time**: Golden hour. Sunset sessions. The monuments become the backdrop.
- **Cost to attend**: Free (Red Bull's DNA — their flagship events are free and open to the public)
- **Fuel station**: Red Bull Zero + Red Bull Organics served at each location. No alcohol. The can IS the party fuel.
